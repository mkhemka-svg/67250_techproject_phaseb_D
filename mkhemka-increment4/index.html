Welcome to the American Museum of Natural History

The American Museum of Natural History (AMNH), located on the Upper West Side of Manhattan, is one of the world's premier natural history institutions. Founded in 1869, the museum spans 21 interconnected buildings with 45 permanent exhibition halls, housing around 32 million specimens and attracting approximately five million visitors each year. Highlights include a 122-foot Titanosaur cast, a T. rex fossil on display since 1915, and an iconic 94-foot blue whale model. In 2023, the museum expanded with the Richard Gilder Center for Science, Education, and Innovation, adding an insectarium, immersive experiences, and expansive collections displays.
